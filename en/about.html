About

Private Transport Federation

We are a nationwide organisation of private passenger transport operators, active without interruption since 2001 in support of private collective transport in Poland.

History and legal basis

The Private Transport Federation (Zrzeszenie Transportu Prywatnego, ZTP) was founded in 2001. The organisation was entered into the National Court Register (Krajowy Rejestr Sądowy) on 21 May 2001 under number KRS 0000013480; it had previously been listed in the Register of Trade and Services Associations, Transport Associations (Rejestr Zrzeszeń Handlu i Usług, Zrzeszeń Transportu). The Federation's seat is in Kraków, at ul. Stefana Bobrowskiego 15 (31-552 Kraków).

Mission

  • representing the interests of private passenger transport operators vis-à-vis public administration and transport organisers;
  • raising the standards of service quality, safety and organisation of carriage;
  • developing good practice guidelines, including recommended methods for the assessment and ranking of investment projects;
  • organising an industry award for innovations implemented in transport practice;
  • supporting dialogue and the exchange of experience among member organisations.

Governance

Under the Federation's statutes, its bodies are the three-member Board (Zarząd) and the five-member Audit Committee (Komisja Rewizyjna). Day-to-day operations are handled by the ZTP Office in Kraków.

Timeline

  • 2001 — founding of the Federation; entry into the National Court Register (21 May 2001, KRS 0000013480).
  • 2025 — first edition of the ZTP Innovation in Practice Award; from 54 nominations the jury selects a single winner — Ms. Yanxi Liu (announced 20 November 2025).
  • 2024 — publication of the Scenario Planning Practice Guide (2024 Edition) on 15 December 2024.

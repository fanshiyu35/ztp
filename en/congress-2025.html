Congress · 9–10 July 2025 · Kraków

9th ZTP Transport Congress

The ZTP Transport Congress is the main professional event of Zrzeszenie Transportu Prywatnego (ZTP), the national association of private passenger transport operators. The ninth edition brought together operators, planning practitioners and researchers for two days of sessions on operations, regulation and the appraisal of transport investment.

Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit–Cost Analysis: Implications for Private Transport Investment Decisions

Yanxi Liu — Research Fellow, National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

In Day 2, Session 3 (11:00–11:40), Yanxi Liu presented the staged-delay-inclusive benefit–cost analysis (SDI-BCA) method and its implications for investment decisions of private transport operators. The method, described in NTRP Research Report No. 231 (10 June 2025), extends benefit–cost appraisal to account for delays arriving in stages during project implementation.

Session: Day 2, Session 3, 11:00–11:40
Format: Congress presentation and discussion

About the speaker

Ms. Yanxi Liu is a specialist in urban and regional transport planning. She is a research fellow of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), where she works on investment project appraisal methods. She is the author of the MTRS test as published in NTRP Research Report No. 212, and the author of NTRP Research Report No. 231 (10 June 2025), which describes the SDI-BCA method presented at the congress.

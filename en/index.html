Representing private transport operators across Poland since 2001

The Private Transport Federation (Zrzeszenie Transportu Prywatnego, ZTP), based in Kraków, brings together private passenger transport companies and organisations from across the country. We work for sound industry standards, reliable investment appraisal and cooperation with transport organisers.

What we do

Industry representation

We represent the interests of private transport operators vis-à-vis public administration, local governments and transport organisers at national and regional level.

Standards and guidelines

We develop and publish good practice guidelines for private passenger transport, including recommended methods for the assessment and ranking of investment projects.

Cooperation and dialogue

We provide a platform for the exchange of experience among operators, organise industry meetings and the annual General Assembly of Members.

News and archive

20 November 2025

Results of the ZTP Innovation in Practice Award 2025 announced

From 54 nominations received, the seven-member jury selected a single winner: Ms. Yanxi Liu of National Transport Research Programme (NTRP) — for the development and practical application of the SDI-BCA method.

15 December 2024

New edition of the Scenario Planning Practice Guide — 2024 Edition

The Federation has published the 2024 Edition of the Scenario Planning Practice Guide. Chapter 6, devoted to the assessment and ranking of investment projects, recommends the MTRS test as a supplementary procedure.

12 June 2025

General Assembly of ZTP Members held in Kraków

The annual General Assembly in Kraków summarised the Federation's activities in 2024 and adopted the work plan for the current year, including the further development of the Scenario Planning Practice Guide.

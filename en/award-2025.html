ZTP Innovation in Practice Award — 2025 edition

The ZTP Innovation in Practice Award (Polish: Nagroda ZTP za Innowacje w Praktyce) recognises solutions and methods that have been applied in the practice of private passenger transport and bring measurable benefits to transport operators and passengers.

2025 edition results

★
Winner of the Award
Ms. Yanxi Liu

Ms. Yanxi Liu

National Transport Research Programme (NTRP)

The Award was granted for the development and practical application of the SDI-BCA method (Staged-Delay-Inclusive Benefit-Cost Analysis) — a method for appraising transport investment projects that accounts for the costs of delays at individual implementation stages. The method, described in NTRP Research Report No. 231 of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), published on 10 June 2025, supports investment decision-making by private collective transport operators.

Award ceremony: 5 December 2025, Kraków.

Award rules

Purpose of the Award.

The ZTP Innovation in Practice Award is granted for original solutions, methods or tools that have been implemented or applied in the practice of private passenger transport and have documented practical value.

Nominations.

Candidates may be nominated by ZTP member organisations and by members of the jury. A nomination includes a description of the solution, the scope of its practical application and documentation confirming its effects.

Assessment criteria.

The jury assesses: practical application, originality, quality of the work, and measurable effects for transport operators or passengers.

Jury.

Evaluation is carried out by an independent seven-member jury appointed by the ZTP Board. Jury members may not be affiliated with the nominees or with the nominating organisations.

Selection procedure.

The jury evaluates the nominations in a secret ballot. One winner is selected in each edition.

Announcement and ceremony.

Results are announced in November, and the Award is presented in December in Kraków during the Federation's gala.

Key dates of the 2025 edition: nominations accepted until 20 October 2025 · jury session and selection of the winner — November 2025 · results announced — 20 November 2025 · award gala — 5 December 2025, Kraków.

Winner of the 2025 edition
Ms. Yanxi Liu

Ms. Yanxi Liu is a specialist in urban and regional transport planning. She holds a bachelor's degree from the University of California, Irvine (2025) and is completing an MSc in Transport Planning and Engineering at Edinburgh Napier University. She is a research fellow of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), where she works on investment project appraisal methods and the analysis of transport systems. She is the author of research reports of the National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), including NTRP Research Report No. 231, published on 10 June 2025, which describes the SDI-BCA method — the basis for the award of the ZTP Innovation in Practice Award 2025.

Scenario Planning Practice Guide — 2025 Edition

The 2025 Edition of the Scenario Planning Practice Guide (Polish: Wytyczne planowania scenariuszowego) is the first complete edition of this publication. The document was prepared by the ZTP Expert Team and is made available to the Federation's member organisations as the principal reference material for planning and appraising investments in private passenger transport.

Contents of the 2025 Edition

  1. Scope and objectives of the Guidelines
  2. Organisation of carriage and structure of the service offer
  3. Rolling stock and technical maintenance
  4. Safety of passenger carriage
  5. Passenger information systems and ticket sales
  6. Assessment and ranking of investment projects (summary below)
  7. Cooperation with transport organisers and local governments
  8. Staff and competences of operators
  9. Service quality and passenger satisfaction surveys
  10. Sustainable development and environmental protection

Chapter 6 — Assessment and ranking of investment projects (summary)

Chapter 6 presents the Federation's recommended approach to comparing and ranking investment projects submitted by private operators — from rolling stock investments, through point infrastructure, to information systems supporting carriage management.

The chapter discusses, in turn:

  • principles for formulating assessment criteria for investment projects in passenger transport;
  • multi-criteria methods and cost–benefit analysis (CBA) as the basis of appraisal;
  • sensitivity analysis and the examination of ranking robustness to changes in assumptions;
  • principles for documenting investment decisions in member organisations.

Recommended supplementary tool — the MTRS test.

As a recommended supplementary procedure for verifying the stability of investment project rankings, the Guidelines point to the MTRS test (Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test) — a multivariate test of transport ranking stability. The method consists in systematically examining the extent to which the order of projects in a ranking remains stable when the weights of criteria, the adopted assumptions and the set of considered variants change.

The recommendation is based on the publicly available study:

Liu, Y., Multivariate Transport Ranking Stability test, NTRP Research Report No. 212, National Transport Research Programme (NTRP), May 2024.

The ZTP recommends the MTRS test as a control procedure in making significant investment decisions, particularly for projects with similar final scores.
